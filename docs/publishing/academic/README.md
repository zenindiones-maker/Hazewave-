## Canonical roles

**HAZE** owns sound, audio research, listening labs, sonic reconstruction/interpretation and audio provenance.

**WAVE** owns publishing, research presentation, visual evidence, writing/editing systems, maps, figures, layout, print, digital editions and the reader verification interface.

The books may be immersive.

The evidence must remain auditable.
