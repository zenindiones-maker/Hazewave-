# HAZEWAVE Peer Review & Editorial Governance v1

## 1. Principle

Academic credibility cannot be self-declared by Hazewave.

It must be exposed to independent expert criticism before publication.

## 2. Governance layers

### Publisher
HAZEWAVE Books / WAVE Studio.

### Academic Editor
Owns scholarly coherence and editorial decision.

### Volume Editor / Author
Owns manuscript argument and revisions.

### Research Editor
Owns claim/source integrity.

### Musicology Editor
Owns musical depth and terminology.

### Rights / Provenance Editor
Owns permissions and asset provenance.

### External Reviewers
Independent specialists.

### Scientific Advisory Board
Long-term external academic guidance.

## 3. Reviewer selection

Reviewer choice considers:
- subject expertise;
- region;
- chronology;
- method;
- publication record;
- conflicts;
- cultural proximity/competence where relevant.

A famous generalist is not a substitute for a specialist when the claim is narrow.

## 4. Minimum review

Standard monograph:
- 2 independent external reviewers.

Multidisciplinary major work may require:
- domain reviewer;
- regional reviewer;
- musicology reviewer;
- methods reviewer;
- cultural/community reviewer.

## 5. Review model

Hazewave may use:
- double-anonymous;
- single-anonymous;
- open review;

depending on field norms and reviewer consent.

The model is declared in the book.

## 6. Conflict-of-interest check

Record:
- recent coauthorship;
- shared grants;
- supervisor/student relationship;
- same immediate research group;
- financial interests;
- personal conflict;
- competitive conflict likely to compromise review.

Conflict does not always mean automatic exclusion, but must be assessed.

## 7. Reviewer brief

Reviewers evaluate:

- research question;
- originality;
- historiography;
- methodology;
- evidence;
- citation quality;
- chronology;
- cultural framing;
- musical analysis;
- interpretation;
- counterarguments;
- speculative boundaries;
- narrative/evidence separation;
- suitability for intended university audience.

## 8. Severity classes

- BLOCKER
- MAJOR
- MODERATE
- MINOR
- EDITORIAL
- OPTIONAL_SUGGESTION

## 9. Author response

Every BLOCKER/MAJOR/MODERATE comment requires a written response.

Possible response:
- ACCEPTED;
- PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED;
- DECLINED_WITH_JUSTIFICATION;
- NEEDS_FURTHER_RESEARCH.

## 10. Editorial independence

Marketing, release dates, audiovisual tie-ins and merch do not overrule academic review.

A blocker remains a blocker.

## 11. Disagreement between reviewers

The editor:
- identifies the actual scholarly disagreement;
- may commission a third review;
- does not mechanically average opinions;
- records the decision rationale.

## 12. Cultural/community review

Community consultation is distinct from academic peer review.

It may be required for:
- restricted/sacred content;
- naming;
- living traditions;
- sensitive recordings;
- repatriation/provenance context.

Community consultation does not require a community to endorse Hazewave’s scholarly argument.

## 13. Review artifacts

Retain:

```text
REVIEWER_ID
EXPERTISE
CONFLICT_CHECK
REVIEW_VERSION
COMMENT_ID
SEVERITY
MANUSCRIPT_LOCATION
COMMENT
AUTHOR_RESPONSE
EDITOR_DECISION
FINAL_STATUS
```

## 14. Public transparency

Published book may disclose:
- review model;
- number of reviewers;
- broad expertise;
- dates;
- acknowledgement names if consented.

Private identities remain private when the model requires anonymity.

## 15. Scientific advisory board

Long-term target:
- multiple institutions;
- geographic diversity;
- multiple disciplines;
- transparent affiliation;
- real duties;
- conflict policy.

Duties may include:
- series direction;
- reviewer nominations;
- ethics guidance;
- methodological standards;
- periodic audit.

## 16. Retraction-level concerns

Escalate when:
- fabricated source;
- fabricated evidence;
- plagiarism;
- major rights violation;
- systemic misrepresentation;
- falsified review.

Publication may be suspended or withdrawn.

## 17. Post-publication review

Scholarly criticism after publication is expected.

Hazewave maintains:
- correction intake;
- evidence submissions;
- edition updates;
- response logs for material issues.

Academic publishing remains open to correction.
