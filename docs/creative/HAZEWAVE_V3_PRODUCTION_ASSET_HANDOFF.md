## Critical sufficiency finding

**8 storyboard illustrations are enough to lock creative direction; they are NOT enough to animate a living scrollytelling site.** Full-scene stills cannot by themselves enable independent physical movement of MPC pads, knobs, cables and platforms. Generating additional entire posters is not a solution. Create *missing parts and states*, while remaining loyal to the approved image's brushwork, spatial geometry and palette.

The animation-production target is:
1. Clean plates with previously occluded pixels restored, not transparent holes.
2. Machines isolated by depth with correct foreground/background occlusion, at least 3 individual MPC rigs.
3. Independent pads (with inactive, energizing and active visual states), faders, knobs and cabling.
4. A single traced sound-wave path in the original composition coordinates, plus causal branch energy strokes and emission masks.
5. Three or more independently drifting translucent nebula/fog planes and depth masks.
6. Hub machine artwork with 3 physically differentiated story poses before Indionesbala branding appears.
7. Articulated transitions between eight storyframes; no fullscreen-poster crossfades presented as animation.
8. Mobile portrait viewport quality at 360×800 and 393×852, touch scroll forward/reverse evidence, 0% path invisible and hub reset on reverse, idle motion, accessible reduced-motion fallback.
9. QR code only after owner-approved PUBLIC HTTPS preview; never a Codespace localhost QR.

### Scrollytelling acceptance

The owner explicitly rejected prior animated posters, shallow pans, generic vector ellipses and decorative light overlays. **If the drawing itself does not animate under scroll, reject.** The sound-wave must physically grow, turn on pads in causal sequence and rearrange mechanical components/occluding atmosphere. Camera movement alone fails. User approval controls the final quality gate. Tests and a green build cannot substitute.
