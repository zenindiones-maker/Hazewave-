## Canonical direction

HAZE = sound.

WAVE = image, animation, publishing and all visual/editorial production.

HAZEWAVE = the coupled creative system.

NEANDERCAUS = the first interactive animated series: a global, era-by-era history of humanity's relationship with sound, from deep prehistory to generative AI, carried by one continuous Brazilian human vocal identity. Music is dialogue; WAVE turns that music into story, animation and interactive visual form.

HAZEWAVE BOOKS = Hazewave's publishing system.

The **HAZEWAVE Academic Library** is the university-level scholarly program. Its books combine deep historical/musicological research with evidence-bound immersive narrative. The reader may live a historical scene through the Bitemporal Lens, but all consequential claims remain traceable to sources and all speculative reconstruction remains explicitly separated from evidence.

HW-ACAD-001 is:

**Música de Antes da Pré-História — O Primeiro Ouvinte: Som, consciência e experiência humana antes da música organizada.**
