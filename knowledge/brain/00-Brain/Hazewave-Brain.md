## Governing principle

Hazewave does not become expert by accumulating prompts.

Professional expertise requires:

1. correct theory;
2. tool operation in the real runtime;
3. objective measurement;
4. judgement between alternatives;
5. recovery and reproducibility;
6. human preference learning when taste is involved.

Knowledge can recommend. Only the Hazewave Harness can authorize execution.

## Daily intelligence loop

`DISCOVER -> VERIFY -> CLASSIFY -> DEDUPLICATE -> CONTRADICTION_CHECK -> SUPERSEDE_STALE -> RECORD_EVIDENCE -> UPDATE_KNOWLEDGE_REGISTRY`

Daily refresh must never auto-install, auto-upgrade, auto-publish or auto-promote a production tool.
